# Device Recovery Procedure

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ADM-006
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: IT administrators

## Purpose and scope

IT needs a consistent response when a managed device is lost.

## Policy

After a lost-device report, IT records the asset ID, last contact, encryption status, and assigned user. IT may lock or wipe the device under the incident lead's direction.

## Procedure

Confirm the reporter's identity, preserve the event log, and tell the security desk what actions were taken. Do not promise that a remote wipe has completed until the system confirms it.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

IT records the time of each lock or wipe command and its confirmed result. The incident lead decides whether a recovered device can be reused.

## Example

IT sends a wipe command to a missing encrypted laptop. The team reports it as completed only after the management system confirms the result.

## Exceptions

If the device is recovered, IT checks its integrity before returning it to service.
