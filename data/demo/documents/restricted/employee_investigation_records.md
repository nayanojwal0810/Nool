# Employee Investigation Records

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ADM-003
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: HR administrators

## Purpose and scope

Investigation files need limited access and a traceable decision.

## Policy

Store witness notes, evidence, and decisions in the restricted case system. Do not place them in shared drives or ordinary email threads.

## Procedure

The case owner records who accessed the file and why, keeps the original evidence, and closes the case only after required review.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The case owner logs each evidence transfer and preserves the original version. A summary shared with a manager must omit details beyond the manager's need.

## Example

A witness note remains in the restricted case system with its original date. The case owner records a later correction without replacing the original evidence.

## Exceptions

A legal hold overrides the normal disposal schedule until the hold is released.
