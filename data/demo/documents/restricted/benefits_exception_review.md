# Benefits Exception Review

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ADM-004
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: HR administrators

## Purpose and scope

Unusual benefits requests need consistent review and a record of the reason.

## Policy

An exception requires written approval from the People Operations lead before a plan change is entered. The reviewer checks the plan rules and the employee's supporting record.

## Procedure

Record the request, decision, approver, effective date, and notice to the employee in the restricted people system.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The reviewer records the plan rule considered and the reason for the decision. People Operations tells the employee the decision without exposing another person's record.

## Example

An employee requests a benefits exception after the normal window. The lead reviews plan rules and supporting records before an authorized change is entered.

## Exceptions

If the request is outside plan terms, refer it to the plan provider before promising an outcome.
