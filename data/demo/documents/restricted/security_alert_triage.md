# Security Alert Triage

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ADM-007
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: IT administrators

## Purpose and scope

Security alerts need an owner and a clear escalation path.

## Policy

The on-call analyst classifies each alert as low, medium, high, or critical and records the reason. High and critical alerts go to the incident lead immediately.

## Procedure

Preserve alert evidence, check affected accounts and devices, and record actions in the restricted incident system. Share only approved facts outside the response team.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The analyst links related alerts under one incident when appropriate. The incident lead decides customer notification and recovery steps for high-risk events.

## Example

An analyst sees several related high alerts and links them under one incident. The incident lead directs the response and approves information shared outside the team.

## Exceptions

If the analyst cannot determine severity, treat the alert as high until the incident lead reviews it.
