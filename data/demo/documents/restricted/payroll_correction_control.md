# Payroll Correction Control

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ADM-002
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: HR administrators

## Purpose and scope

Payroll changes require evidence and a second check before release.

## Policy

A payroll correction needs a source record and approval from a second authorized People Operations reviewer. The reviewer cannot be the person who entered the change.

## Procedure

Record the old and new value, reason, effective period, and approver in the restricted payroll system. Tell the employee through a private channel after verification.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The second reviewer compares the change with the source evidence and payroll period. The system records both identities and the approval time.

## Example

An HR specialist enters a correction but cannot approve their own entry. A second reviewer compares the change to the source record before payroll release.

## Exceptions

Urgent corrections follow the same two-person check before payroll release.
