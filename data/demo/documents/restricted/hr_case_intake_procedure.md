# HR Case Intake Procedure

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ADM-001
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: HR administrators

## Purpose and scope

People Operations needs a controlled record for sensitive employee concerns.

## Policy

Create a restricted case record for each concern and assign one case owner. Share case details only with approved reviewers who need them for the investigation.

## Procedure

Record the report date, source, summary, and next action in the restricted people system. Acknowledge receipt privately and set a review date.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The case owner sets the next review date and documents each handoff. Restricted access remains limited even after the case is closed.

## Example

A case owner records the first report privately and sets a review date. If the report names that owner, another authorized reviewer takes the case.

## Exceptions

If the report involves the assigned owner, transfer the case to another authorized HR reviewer.
