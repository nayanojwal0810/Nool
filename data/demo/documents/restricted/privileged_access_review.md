# Privileged Access Review

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ADM-005
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: IT administrators

## Purpose and scope

Administrative system access needs a regular independent review.

## Policy

IT reviews privileged accounts every quarter. A system owner confirms each user's business need and IT removes access that is no longer approved.

## Procedure

Export the account list from the managed system, record owner decisions, and keep evidence of removals in the restricted access register.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The access register records each owner decision and the date IT removed access. The next review checks any account that remained open under an exception.

## Example

At quarterly review, a system owner finds an administrator who changed roles. IT removes the old privilege and records the owner decision.

## Exceptions

A temporary privileged account must have an expiry date and is reviewed at expiry even between quarterly reviews.
