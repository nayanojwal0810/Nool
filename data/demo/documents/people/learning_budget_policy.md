# Learning Budget Policy

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-008
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need a fair way to request training linked to their work.

## Policy

The annual learning allowance is INR 20,000 per employee, subject to manager approval and available team budget. Courses must relate to a current role or agreed development goal.

## Procedure

Send the course description and cost to the manager before purchase. After approval, book through the purchasing process and share completion evidence with People Operations.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Approval must happen before enrollment or payment. The employee shares proof of completion so the team can evaluate whether the course supported the agreed goal.

## Example

An employee asks for a job-related course costing INR 18,000. The manager checks the development goal and budget before the employee enrolls.

## Exceptions

A course above the allowance needs department head and Finance approval before any commitment.
