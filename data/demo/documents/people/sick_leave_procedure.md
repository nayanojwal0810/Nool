# Sick Leave Procedure

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-002
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need a clear route to report illness without sharing unnecessary medical details.

## Policy

Employees may take up to ten paid sick days each calendar year. A medical certificate is needed when an absence lasts more than three consecutive workdays.

## Procedure

Notify the line manager before the normal start time when possible and record the absence in the people portal. People Operations records the dates, not a diagnosis.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Managers plan cover without asking for a diagnosis. People Operations records any certificate in the restricted employee record and tells the employee if more information is needed.

## Example

An employee who is ill on Monday tells the manager before work starts, then enters the absence when able. People Operations asks for a certificate only if the absence passes three consecutive workdays.

## Exceptions

If an employee cannot report personally, a family member may notify the manager; People Operations follows up privately.
