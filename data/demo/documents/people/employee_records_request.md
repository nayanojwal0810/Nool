# Employee Records Request

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-009
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need a safe way to view or correct their own personnel records.

## Policy

Employees may request a copy or correction of their own employment records. People Operations verifies identity before releasing a record and responds within ten business days.

## Procedure

Submit the request through the private people portal form and describe the record or correction needed. Do not send identity documents through a public chat or team channel.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

People Operations confirms which record was changed or why a requested correction was declined. The request and decision remain in the restricted employee record.

## Example

If a start date appears incorrectly in a record, the employee uses the private form and describes the correct date. People Operations verifies the source before changing it.

## Exceptions

If a record is disputed, People Operations keeps the original and the employee's statement while reviewing the change.
