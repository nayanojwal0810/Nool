# Workplace Conduct Policy

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-005
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need a clear standard for respectful conduct in offices and online meetings.

## Policy

Treat colleagues, customers, and vendors with respect. Harassment, retaliation, and sharing confidential personnel information are prohibited.

## Procedure

Raise a concern with a manager or People Operations. If the manager is involved, contact People Operations directly through the confidential channel.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

People Operations records concerns in a restricted case file and shares them only with reviewers who need the information. A person who reports a concern must not face retaliation.

## Example

If a colleague reports a concern about their manager, People Operations receives the report directly. The team protects the report from unnecessary circulation while it reviews the facts.

## Exceptions

People Operations may arrange an immediate safety measure while a concern is reviewed.
