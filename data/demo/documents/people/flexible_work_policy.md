# Flexible Work Policy

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-004
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Teams need a shared rule for working away from the office while remaining available.

## Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time.

## Procedure

Agree regular remote days with the manager and record them in the team calendar. Use a company-managed device and an approved network connection.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Employees remain reachable during core hours and tell the team when connectivity changes. Remote work does not change the device and data handling rules in the IT policies.

## Example

A team may agree on Tuesday and Thursday as regular remote days. The employee still attends an on-site customer workshop when the manager gives reasonable notice.

## Exceptions

Managers may change a remote day for a required on-site activity with reasonable notice.
