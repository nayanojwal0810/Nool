# Benefits Enrollment Guide

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-007
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need to know when to enroll and how to change benefits after a life event.

## Policy

New employees select benefits within 30 calendar days of joining. Existing employees may change selections during the annual enrollment window or after an eligible life event.

## Procedure

Review the current plan summary in the people portal, choose options, and confirm the submission receipt. People Operations can explain enrollment steps but does not make selections for employees.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The plan summary controls coverage and eligibility. Employees should check the effective date shown in the portal before relying on a new selection.

## Example

A new starter reviews plan details during the first month and confirms the choice in the portal. A later family change is reported within the separate life-event window.

## Exceptions

Report a qualifying life event to People Operations within 30 calendar days for a midyear change.
