# Public Holiday Calendar

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-003
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Teams need one source for company holidays and regional office closures.

## Policy

People Operations publishes the annual holiday calendar by 1 December for the next year. Employees follow the calendar assigned to their work location.

## Procedure

Check the people portal before booking leave or travel. Managers confirm coverage for customer-facing work on regional holidays.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The calendar identifies each location and the observed date when a holiday falls on a weekend. A manager should use the published calendar when planning customer coverage.

## Example

An employee who moves to another office checks the calendar for the new location. The manager confirms which regional holidays apply from the move date.

## Exceptions

If business work is required on a listed holiday, the manager arranges a substitute day with People Operations.
