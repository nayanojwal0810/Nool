# Performance Review Guide

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-006
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees and managers need a consistent review schedule and record of goals.

## Policy

Formal reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review covers agreed goals, examples of work, and development needs.

## Procedure

The manager schedules a meeting, shares written feedback, and records agreed actions in the people portal. The employee can add comments before the review closes.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Employees may attach work examples and comment on the written review. Managers distinguish observed results from future goals and set a date to check development actions.

## Example

Before the June meeting, the employee gathers examples against agreed goals. The manager records clear actions and a follow-up date instead of relying on an informal conversation.

## Exceptions

If a review is delayed, the manager tells the employee and People Operations the revised date.
