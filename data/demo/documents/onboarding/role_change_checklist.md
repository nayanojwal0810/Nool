# Role Change Checklist

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ONB-004
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Role changes may require different system access and responsibilities.

## Policy

The receiving manager starts a role change request at least five business days before the change. Old access must be reviewed and removed when no longer needed.

## Procedure

People Operations updates the role record. IT compares the old and new access sets, obtains approvals, and records the effective date.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The old manager confirms work handover; the new manager confirms the access needed for the new role. IT records removals as well as additions.

## Example

An employee moving from support to finance needs some access added and other access removed. Both managers confirm the handover before IT changes permissions.

## Exceptions

An urgent transfer may start sooner, but the manager must arrange a same-day access review.
