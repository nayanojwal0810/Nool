# Equipment Handover

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ONB-003
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Company equipment must have a clear owner from issue to return.

## Policy

IT records the asset number and assigned user before releasing a laptop or phone. The employee confirms receipt and reports damage or loss promptly.

## Procedure

Check the device and accessories at handover. Keep the handover receipt and return all listed items during a role change or exit.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Both IT and the employee keep the handover receipt. The asset number follows the device through repair, reassignment, and return.

## Example

At handover, IT and the employee confirm the laptop and charger on one receipt. The same asset record is checked when the laptop is returned.

## Exceptions

IT documents missing items and refers any cost decision to People Operations; the help desk does not make payroll deductions.
