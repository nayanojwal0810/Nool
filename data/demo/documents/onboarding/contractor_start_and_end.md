# Contractor Start and End

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ONB-006
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Temporary workers need access limited to approved work and dates.

## Policy

A sponsor requests contractor access with a named owner and end date. Access ends automatically on the approved date unless the sponsor renews it.

## Procedure

The sponsor confirms the contract, required systems, and data classification before IT grants access. Review the account before extending the engagement.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The sponsor reviews contractor access every month during the engagement. IT removes access at expiry even if the sponsor has not completed a renewal request.

## Example

A contractor working through June has an approved June end date. The sponsor asks for an extension before expiry if the work continues.

## Exceptions

Emergency extensions require the sponsor and IT to record the reason and a new expiry date.
