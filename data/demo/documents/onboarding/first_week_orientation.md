# First Week Orientation

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ONB-002
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

New employees need a clear set of first-week activities.

## Policy

During the first week, complete identity verification, security training, payroll details, and a meeting with the line manager. Do not use a colleague's account while waiting for access.

## Procedure

People Operations provides the orientation schedule. The manager confirms role goals, and the employee records completed activities in the people portal.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Security training must be completed before the employee handles Restricted information. The manager checks completion in the people portal at the end of the week.

## Example

A new employee without restricted-system access can complete orientation and meet the team. The manager does not ask them to use a colleague's account.

## Exceptions

If access is late, the manager arranges work that does not require restricted systems.
