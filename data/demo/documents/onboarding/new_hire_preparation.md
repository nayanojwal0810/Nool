# New Hire Preparation

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ONB-001
Owner: People Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Teams need equipment and access ready for a new employee's first day.

## Policy

The hiring manager completes the new hire checklist at least five business days before the start date. People Operations confirms joining details and IT receives the access request.

## Procedure

Confirm the work location, manager, start time, equipment needs, and role. IT provisions only the access approved for that role.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The manager names a buddy and shares the first-week schedule with the new employee. People Operations confirms that the person knows where to report on day one.

## Example

For a new analyst, the manager confirms location and laptop needs early. People Operations and IT receive the same start date so equipment and access are ready together.

## Exceptions

A late change to the start date must be sent to People Operations and IT as soon as it is known.
