# Corporate Card Use

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-007
Owner: Finance
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Company cards should pay only for authorized business costs.

## Policy

Only the named cardholder may use a corporate card, and every transaction needs a receipt and business purpose. Cash withdrawals and personal purchases are prohibited.

## Procedure

Upload receipts within five business days and match each transaction in the expense portal. Report a lost card to Finance and the card provider immediately.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The cardholder reviews transactions each week and disputes an unknown charge promptly. Finance retains the receipt and approval trail with the card record.

## Example

A cardholder sees an unfamiliar transaction during weekly review. They tell Finance and the card provider promptly, then preserve the transaction record for review.

## Exceptions

Finance may suspend a card while missing transactions or suspected misuse are reviewed.
