# Client Hospitality Rule

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-006
Owner: Finance
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Client meals and events need a business reason and advance approval.

## Policy

A client hospitality expense needs manager approval before booking. Any single event above INR 15,000 also needs Finance approval.

## Procedure

Record the client organization, attendees, purpose, estimated cost, and approval in the expense request. Submit itemized receipts after the event.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The host records all attendees, including employees and clients. Finance may reject an event claim when the prior approval or business purpose is missing.

## Example

A client dinner estimated at INR 18,000 needs manager and Finance approval before booking. The host records attendees and keeps the itemized receipt afterward.

## Exceptions

Gifts and cash equivalents are not covered by this rule and require separate compliance review.
