# Vendor Invoice Handling

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-005
Owner: Finance
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Invoices must match approved work before Finance pays a vendor.

## Policy

Send vendor invoices to the Finance intake address with a purchase order number. The business owner confirms delivery before payment.

## Procedure

Check the invoice name, dates, currency, tax details, and amount against the purchase order. Report any mismatch to Finance before confirming receipt.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The business owner checks that the billed goods or service arrived as agreed. Finance matches the invoice to the purchase order and the receipt confirmation.

## Example

A vendor submits an invoice for delivered equipment. The business owner confirms receipt, and Finance checks the invoice against the purchase order before payment.

## Exceptions

Finance holds disputed invoices until the business owner and vendor resolve the difference.
