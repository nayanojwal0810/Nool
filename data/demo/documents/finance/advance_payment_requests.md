# Advance Payment Requests

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-008
Owner: Finance
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Some approved work needs payment before delivery.

## Policy

Request an advance payment at least five business days before it is needed. The request must identify the vendor, contract or purchase order, amount, and planned settlement date.

## Procedure

The manager checks the business need and Finance checks the supporting records before release. The owner submits final receipts and reconciles unused funds within ten business days after the event.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

An advance is not an extra allowance. Finance reconciles it against receipts and asks for unused funds to be returned after the activity ends.

## Example

For a workshop deposit, the owner requests the advance with the approved order and settlement date. After the workshop, Finance compares receipts with the released amount.

## Exceptions

Finance may decline an advance when supporting documents are missing.
