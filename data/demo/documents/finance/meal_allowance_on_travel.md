# Meal Allowance on Travel

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-003
Owner: Finance
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Travelers need a clear daily meal limit.

## Policy

The daily meal limit for approved domestic business travel is INR 1,200 per person. Alcohol and meals for companions are excluded.

## Procedure

Keep itemized receipts and enter meals under the travel claim for the relevant date. Do not claim a meal already provided by a conference or hotel.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The allowance is a ceiling, not an automatic payment. Finance reimburses actual eligible costs shown on receipts up to the daily limit.

## Example

If a traveler spends INR 900 on eligible meals, the claim is INR 900 with receipts. The INR 1,200 limit does not create an extra INR 300 payment.

## Exceptions

A client meal follows the separate client hospitality approval rule, not the individual meal allowance.
