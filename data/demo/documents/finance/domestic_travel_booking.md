# Domestic Travel Booking

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-002
Owner: Finance
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Business travel should be approved and booked through one process.

## Policy

Request domestic travel at least seven calendar days before departure. Book transport and lodging through the company travel portal after the manager approves the trip.

## Procedure

Record the business purpose, destination, dates, and cost center in the request. Keep the booking confirmation for the later expense claim.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Travelers should use the approved fare and lodging options shown in the portal. They must get approval before booking a personal extension or a higher-cost option.

## Example

A traveler requests a customer visit eight days ahead, waits for approval, then chooses an approved portal option. An urgent next-day visit needs written exception approval.

## Exceptions

Urgent travel can use a shorter notice period with written manager and Finance approval.
