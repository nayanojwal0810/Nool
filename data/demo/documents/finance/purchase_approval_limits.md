# Purchase Approval Limits

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-004
Owner: Finance
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need approval before making a company purchase.

## Policy

A manager may approve a purchase up to INR 25,000. Purchases above INR 25,000 also need Finance approval. A department director must additionally approve amounts above INR 100,000.

## Procedure

Submit the quote, business reason, vendor, and cost center before ordering. The purchasing team records the approvals and issues a purchase order.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Approvals must be recorded before an order is placed. The purchasing team checks whether related items form one purchase when applying the threshold.

## Example

A purchase of INR 120,000 needs manager, Finance, and director approval before the order. Dividing it into two INR 60,000 orders does not change the approval level.

## Exceptions

Do not split one purchase into smaller orders to avoid an approval level.
