# Managed Device Policy

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: IT-003
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Company devices must remain protected and traceable throughout their use.

## Policy

Work with company data on an enrolled, company-managed device. Keep automatic updates and screen lock enabled; do not disable endpoint protection.

## Procedure

Report a damaged or missing device through the service desk. IT records the asset number, checks its status, and arranges repair or replacement.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The device remains company property and must be returned at exit. IT checks the device record before repair, replacement, or reassignment.

## Example

When a laptop fails, the employee reports its asset number. IT checks the device record and decides whether to repair or replace it before assigning another device.

## Exceptions

Personal devices require a documented exception from IT before they access company data.
