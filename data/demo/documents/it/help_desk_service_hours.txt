# Help Desk Service Hours

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: IT-009
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Employees need a clear support route and response expectations.

POLICY
The service desk handles normal requests from 09:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time on business days. Critical security incidents use the emergency line at any time.

PROCEDURE
Open a ticket for routine access or device problems and include the affected service and error. For a critical incident, call the emergency line instead of waiting for a ticket response.

RESPONSIBILITIES AND FOLLOW-UP
Routine requests receive a ticket number for follow-up. The emergency line is reserved for active security incidents and major service outages.

EXAMPLE
A routine printer fault goes into the ticket queue during service hours. A suspected account compromise uses the emergency route even outside those hours.

EXCEPTIONS
Holiday coverage follows the on-call rota published in the service desk portal.
