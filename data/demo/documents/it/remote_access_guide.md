# Remote Access Guide

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: IT-004
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Remote workers need a protected path to internal systems.

## Policy

Use the approved VPN when accessing internal systems outside a company office. Connect from a trusted network and do not use a shared public computer.

## Procedure

Open the VPN client, sign in with MFA, and check its connected status before opening internal tools. Contact the service desk if access fails.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

A VPN connection protects the route to internal services but does not make an unapproved device acceptable. Employees should disconnect when work is complete.

## Example

An employee working from a home network connects the VPN before opening an internal finance tool. A failed VPN connection is reported instead of bypassed.

## Exceptions

If the VPN is unavailable, stop work that needs internal systems and use only approved public services until IT restores access.
