# Password and MFA Standard

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: IT-002
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need a consistent way to protect company accounts.

## Policy

Use a unique passphrase of at least 14 characters for company accounts and enable multi-factor authentication. Do not share passwords or approve an MFA prompt you did not initiate.

## Procedure

Use the approved password manager to store credentials. Report an unexpected MFA prompt to the service desk and change the affected password immediately.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

IT never asks an employee to disclose a password. An employee who approved an unexpected MFA request must report it as a possible account incident.

## Example

An unexpected MFA prompt is a warning even if the employee did not type a password. The employee declines the prompt and alerts the service desk.

## Exceptions

IT may issue a temporary recovery method after identity verification; it expires after the recovery session.
