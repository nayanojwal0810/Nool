# Account Provisioning Guide

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: IT-001
Owner: Information Technology
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

New staff need approved access before they can use company systems.

## Policy

A manager requests a standard account at least three business days before a new employee starts. Access is based on the employee's role and recorded approval.

## Procedure

The manager submits the request in the service desk portal. IT confirms the manager, assigns the standard access set, and sends activation instructions to the employee.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

IT records the approver, role, systems, and issue date in the access register. Managers review the access when a person changes role or leaves.

## Example

For a Monday start, the manager submits the account request by the preceding Wednesday. IT records the approval and gives the new employee only the standard access for the role.

## Exceptions

Urgent access requests require the manager to state the business reason and expected end date.
