# Meeting and Decision Records

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: OPS-001
Owner: Workplace Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Teams need a reliable record of decisions and owners.

## Policy

For project decisions, the meeting owner records the decision, date, responsible person, and due date in the team workspace within two business days.

## Procedure

Share the record with attendees and link related files. Correct factual errors in the record rather than creating conflicting copies.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The owner marks open actions separately from final decisions. A later change should link to the earlier decision so readers can see what was replaced.

## Example

After a project meeting chooses a vendor, the owner records who decided, why, and who will act. Attendees can then find the decision without relying on memory.

## Exceptions

Sensitive personnel matters belong in the restricted people system, not the team workspace.
