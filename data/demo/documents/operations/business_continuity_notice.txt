# Business Continuity Notice

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: OPS-006
Owner: Workplace Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Teams need a simple response when an office or service is unavailable.

POLICY
During a declared disruption, follow the notice from the incident lead. Managers confirm staff safety and move approved work to the published alternate location or remote arrangement.

PROCEDURE
Check the company status page, tell the manager if you cannot work safely, and avoid sharing unverified updates externally.

RESPONSIBILITIES AND FOLLOW-UP
Managers confirm which work can continue and where staff should report. Employees should use the status page for updates rather than forwarding unverified messages.

EXAMPLE
If an office loses power, staff check the status page and contact their manager about safe work. Only the incident lead confirms when normal work can resume.

EXCEPTIONS
Only the incident lead may declare a return to normal operations.
