# Visitor Access Procedure

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: OPS-004
Owner: Workplace Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Office visitors must be expected and accompanied.

## Policy

The host registers a visitor at least one business day before arrival. Visitors show identification at reception and wear a visitor badge while on site.

## Procedure

The host meets the visitor, escorts them in work areas, and returns the badge at departure. Do not give a visitor an employee access card.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

Reception records arrival and departure. The host remains responsible for the visitor in non-public areas and reports a missing badge immediately.

## Example

A customer visiting tomorrow is registered today. The host meets them at reception and keeps them accompanied in work areas.

## Exceptions

For an unplanned visitor, reception contacts the host and waits for approval before entry.
