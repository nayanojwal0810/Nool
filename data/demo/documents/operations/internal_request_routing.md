# Internal Request Routing

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: OPS-005
Owner: Workplace Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Employees need to send requests to the team that can resolve them.

## Policy

Use the service desk for IT issues, the people portal for employment matters, the expense portal for claims, and the facilities portal for building requests.

## Procedure

Choose the closest category, include the needed facts, and keep the request number for follow-up. Do not place personal or customer secrets in a general request.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The assigned team owns the request until it is resolved or formally transferred. Requesters can use the number to check progress without filing a duplicate.

## Example

A laptop fault belongs with the service desk, while a pay-record question belongs in the people portal. The receiving team transfers a misrouted request.

## Exceptions

If a request covers two teams, the receiving owner coordinates a transfer rather than asking the employee to start again.
