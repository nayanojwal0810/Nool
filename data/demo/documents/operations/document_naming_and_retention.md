# Document Naming and Retention

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: OPS-002
Owner: Workplace Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

Staff need to find current files and avoid accidental use of outdated copies.

## Policy

Use a descriptive title, owner, and version for shared procedures. Keep the current copy in the approved team workspace and archive superseded copies.

## Procedure

When updating a procedure, change the version and effective date, identify the owner, and link the new copy from the team index.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The document owner checks links after publishing a new version. Teams should not keep an unmarked local copy as their source of current instructions.

## Example

When a procedure changes, its owner publishes the new version and updates the team index. The old copy is marked as superseded so staff do not follow it by mistake.

## Exceptions

Ask the document owner before deleting a record under a legal or customer hold.
