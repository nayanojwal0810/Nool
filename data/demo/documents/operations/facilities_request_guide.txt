# Facilities Request Guide

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: OPS-003
Owner: Workplace Operations
Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Audience: All employees

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Employees need one route for office repairs and access issues.

POLICY
Open a facilities request for non-urgent repairs, seating, or building access. For an immediate safety concern, call the front desk emergency number.

PROCEDURE
Describe the location, issue, and impact in the facilities portal. The facilities team assigns an owner and updates the request when work is scheduled.

RESPONSIBILITIES AND FOLLOW-UP
Facilities gives the requester a reference number and an expected update time. For a safety concern, staff should move away from the affected area first.

EXAMPLE
A loose cable in a walkway is an immediate safety issue. The employee moves away and calls reception; a routine desk repair can use the facilities portal.

EXCEPTIONS
Do not attempt electrical or building repairs without facilities authorization.
