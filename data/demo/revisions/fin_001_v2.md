# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: FIN-001
Owner: Finance
Version: 2
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Audience: All employees

## Policy

Submit an expense claim within 45 calendar days of the purchase. Every claim needs a receipt and a business purpose; personal purchases are not reimbursable.

## Change notice

This version replaces the previous rule. Follow the current policy and contact Finance for a question about an earlier claim or request.
