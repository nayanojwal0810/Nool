# Annual Leave Policy

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: PEO-001
Owner: People Operations
Version: 2
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Audience: All employees

## Policy

Full-time employees receive 22 paid annual leave days each calendar year. Carry over remains limited to five days, to be used by 31 March.

## Change notice

This version replaces the previous rule. Follow the current policy and contact People Operations for a question about an earlier claim or request.
