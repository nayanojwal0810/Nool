# Employee Exit Procedure

Lindenmere Services
Document ID: ONB-005
Owner: People Operations
Version: 2
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Audience: All employees

## Purpose and scope

A planned exit needs coordinated handover and account closure.

## Policy

The manager records the last working day with People Operations and starts the exit checklist. IT disables access by 18:00 India Standard Time on that day and tracks returned assets.

## Procedure

The employee hands over work, returns company equipment, and confirms expense claims before the last day. The manager checks ownership of shared files and open customer work.

## Responsibilities and follow-up

The manager transfers ownership of shared work before access closes. People Operations records the completed checklist and any unresolved asset return.

## Example

Before the last working day, the manager assigns ownership of shared customer files. IT closes access at the approved time and records returned devices.

## Exceptions

For an unplanned exit, People Operations and IT coordinate an immediate access decision.
